BOUNDED, SEPARATE-SIGN, PAPER-INSPIRED WAKE SENSITIVITY

This is a new local candidate, not a change to the original analysis. It is
not Efficient Wake, a shower-level implementation of that paper, or a calibrated
theoretical uncertainty. No cluster rerun is implied by creating these files.

Paper input retained
-------------------
Reuse the existing fit to Fig.6 of arXiv:2609.10679v1 and its massless signed
Hybrid reference (arXiv:1609.05842). The frozen 50-GeV fit supplies positive/
negative pT scales h_s(a), positive azimuth/rapidity contractions and population
amplitudes. a is ONE global scalar in [0,1], shared across design points and
observables. The frozen calibration and log-interpolation path are unchanged.
The paper's negative angular region is a net deficit; it is not by itself a
measurement of the generator's separately tagged negative-particle population.
Transferring that signed template to our two finite particle pools is an
additional ansatz, not a published particle-by-particle prescription.

The particle-level TRANSVERSE ENVELOPE is now a hard implementation constraint:
  pT_final_i <= h_sign(a) * pT_original_i.
This is an envelope derived from our fitted proposal, NOT a universal QCD bound
or a claim that the paper prescribes this finite-event transfer rule. There is
no arbitrary absolute pT cap, clipping of a finished event, or hard recoil.

Budget and population selection
-------------------------------
Pminus denotes the unsigned positive-energy four-vector of negative wake rows.
For a chosen fraction f, set
  target_minus = f*Pminus;
  target_plus  = Pplus-Pminus+target_minus.
The signed difference is exactly the original wake four-vector. f<=1 bounds
both unsigned sign energies by their original energies, in every frame.
The requested f is the existing negative amplitude times h_minus.

Use charge-balanced thinning with the existing amplitudes, conditional on >=2
rows when a sign originally has >=2. Unmatched species charges are protected;
an original singleton remains present. The conditioning is sampled exactly,
not through an arbitrary number of rejected random draws. The same event seed
and random stream are reused for matching event fingerprints. At different a
the original sampling units match; across designs different particle populations
need not have a one-to-one unit match. Cross-design and cross-a noise is measured
from the paired event rows, not assumed independent or perfectly correlated.

Priority is EXPLICIT:
1. Try the requested f with that selected population.
2. If f=1 is feasible, find the nearest feasible f>=requested within THAT
   population using the convex feasibility interval.
3. Otherwise restore ALL omitted rows in the eligible wake and repeat. This
   deterministic restoration changes the finite-population sampling law and is
   recorded. We do not claim a globally optimal population or globally closest f.

An original singleton or absent positive sign can force f=1. That does NOT
silently bypass the entire event: the other sign is still transformed subject
to conservation and its pT envelope. No failed whole event is dropped or replaced
by an unreported identity result. Inherited no-hard-axis / <8-eligible-row cases
retain the original event and explicit QA flags. Spacelike input rows and exact
zero-transverse-mass rows are retained bytewise, not repaired.

Why a feasible endpoint exists
------------------------------
Restoring all eligible original particles and f=1 permits d_s=1/h_s, which
recovers their original pT. Their original angles/rapidities then give an exact
existence certificate with unchanged masses/charges. Failure to find any valid
conserving state is still fatal: existence is not an excuse to accept a bad
numerical answer. An optional analytic seed may fail without invalidating an
already conserved Newton solution.

Bounded per-sign construction
----------------------------
Work in one common longitudinal frame only. For each sign choose a common
downward pT multiplier d_s<=1. It multiplies the proposed h_s*pT_original.
Allowed d obey both the transverse polygon bounds and
  sum_i sqrt(m_i^2 + d_s^2*cap_i^2) <= sqrt(E_target^2-Pz_target^2).
Singletons require equality, not just this inequality; zero-mT padding cannot
create an extra longitudinal degree of freedom.

Within the allowed d interval, match the proposed longitudinal invariant mass
when possible. Then only a common rapidity boost is needed, preserving proposed
rapidity spread. Otherwise choose its closest endpoint and solve the required
rapidity spread explicitly; this distortion is measured, not declared absent.
Azimuth angles solve their independent transverse polygon. A bounded Newton
start and a constructive geometric start are compared, then a few conservative
null-space descent steps reduce angular displacement without changing pT or the
target. This is not a global-minimum claim.

For sub-ULP transverse polygon sides, the analytic constructor first retains
their proposed azimuths and subtracts their FULL vector sum from the resolved
polygon target. Those particles and momenta are not removed or set to zero.
The recombined full vector is verified against the same conservation tolerance.
This only handles floating-point geometry; it does not loosen the pT envelope,
mass-shell or four-momentum checks.

The final float64 rows, which native reconstruction actually receives, must
satisfy the original mass-shell and signed-four-momentum tolerances, positive
unsigned sign energies, species charges, unchanged non-wake rows and rapidity
guard. Each final pT is checked against its own envelope. No unrestricted
three-momentum or sign-rest-frame energy redistribution is allowed.

What is and is not guaranteed
----------------------------
The particlewise envelope prevents the previous 100--353-GeV artificial wake
tail when the original scaled particles are soft. It does NOT prove that all
possible inputs have pT<10/30 GeV, prevent collective jet shifts, certify native
grooming for every design, or establish hydrodynamic shape fidelity.

The actual h_s*d_s may be <1, especially in negative sectors with a few massive,
charge-protected particles. Negative hardening, fitted amplitudes and proposed
angular shapes are NOT promised after exact finite-event conservation. Those
differences must be shown alongside results; a means requested deformation,
not exact agreement with the published massless single-parton template.

Nearest outgoing hard-parton axes are still a transfer assumption because no
true wake deposition ancestry is stored. Missing joint/species information,
finite massive populations, the separate-sign budget ansatz and conditional
restoration remain model choices. Even with nuisance marginalization this is
only a sensitivity test.

Local validation and plotting
-----------------------------
The source-pinned local runner uses identical events across nodes and methods,
unchanged native reconstruction, original shared pp moments, full-production
cross sections and established centrality normalizations. It stores paired
counts, full across-node/observable covariance, exact untruncated profile
moments, and binned profiles. It checks covariance PSD without a PSD projection,
bitwise original baseline reproduction and unchanged hadron counts on the local
sample whose charged-wake envelope is below10GeV.

Full comparison: original, previous joint projection, proposed shape, and new
bounded map. Show positive/negative pT and angular profiles, high-pT tails,
population restoration, f and d changes, conservation residuals and observable
responses. Simulation bands are MC errors, not posterior or theory-error bands.
Native guard failures stop the test; none are assigned guessed splitting bins.

Old rejected sources/results remain untouched. Main tables, emulators, priors,
PC choices, experimental covariance and inference are not changed by this test.
